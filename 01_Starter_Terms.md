# Welcome to Good Kicks ambassadors.

Glad to have you. Here's how this works.

---

## What you get

- **1 free Good Kicks sac**, your colorway choice, shipped to you.
- **A personal discount code** (e.g. `MILTONHIGH15`) that gives your followers 15% off at goodkicks.co.
- **8% commission** on every order placed with your code. Paid monthly via Venmo or PayPal.
- **Reposts of your content** on our main account when you tag us.
- **First access** to V2 colorways, drops, and any future custom collabs.
- **A path to leveling up** — post regularly and your code will drive orders. As you do, you climb to Repping (20% code, 9% commission, 2 more sacs) and Anchor (25% code, 10% commission, 6 more sacs, custom colorway possible).

---

## What we ask — three things, always

These three are baseline requirements at every tier (Starter, Repping, Anchor). If you can't commit to all three, the program isn't the right fit — and that's totally fine.

### 1. Link to goodkicks.co in your Instagram bio

A direct link to our site, always. Linktree is fine if you have multiple links — just make sure goodkicks.co is one of them and easy to spot.

### 2. Your personal discount code in your bio

Include your code (e.g. `MILTONHIGH15`) somewhere in your bio text or in a pinned story highlight where followers can find it. The point: anyone scrolling your profile can grab it without asking.

### 3. @goodkicks mention in every post or reel where the sac is visible

Caption mention OR verbal shoutout — your call. Examples:
- Caption: "good kick around today 🤝 @goodkicks"
- Verbal: "shoutout @goodkicks for the sac" in the audio of a reel
- Tag: tagging @goodkicks in the post itself

Just mention us somehow on every post where the sac shows up. Doesn't matter if the sac is the focus or just in the background — if it's on camera, we get a mention.

**FTC disclosure:** include `#gifted` or write "sent by @goodkicks" / "ambassador" in the caption of any post where you got the sac for free. Not optional — it's federal law for sponsored content, even at small scale.

---

## What we DON'T ask

- We don't ask you to post on a schedule.
- We don't ask you to post a specific number of times per month.
- We don't ask you to say anything specific in your captions (other than the @goodkicks mention above).
- We don't ask for any payment, signup fee, or commitment up front.
- We don't ask you to sign a contract.

You decide what your content looks like. We just ask that whenever the sac shows up, we're credited.

## How tier upgrades work

Three tiers. You start at Starter. Move up based on activity:

| Tier | How you qualify | Customer code | Commission | Free product |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Sign up — automatic on approval | 15% off | 8% per order | 1 free sac |
| **Repping** | 5+ tagged posts OR 5+ code redemptions | 20% off | 9% per order | +2 free sacs, monthly resupply |
| **Anchor** | 15+ posts OR 25+ redemptions | 25% off | 10% per order | +6 sacs, named partnership, custom colorway possible |

We track this manually right now. When you hit a milestone, DM us and we'll bump your tier and update your code.

The three baseline requirements above (bio link, bio code, mention on visible posts) apply at every tier. Strictly. If you stop following them, we'll send 1-2 friendly reminders. If they're still missing after that, your code gets deactivated — no hard feelings, just a structural reset.

---

## How commissions work

- **Earned on every order** placed using your code at goodkicks.co.
- **Calculated on the discounted subtotal** (so on a $14.40 order using a 20% code, your commission is 9% × $14.40 = $1.30).
- **Paid monthly** via Venmo or PayPal.
- **You give us your payment info AFTER you hit the Repping tier** — keeping things simple at Starter so there's no payment paperwork before you've actually earned anything.
- **No minimum payout threshold** — if you've earned $2 this month, we send $2.
- **1099 form at year-end** if your total commissions cross $600 in a calendar year (IRS requirement).

---

## How long this lasts

- Your code stays active as long as you're meeting the three baseline requirements AND posting at least once every 90 days. After 90 days of inactivity, the code auto-expires (you can re-earn it by posting again).
- There's no end date. You're an ambassador as long as you want to be.
- You can step away anytime by just telling us — no awkward exit, no penalty. We'll deactivate your code, settle your last commission payout, and you keep your sac.

---

## Important fine print

- This is a partnership of trust, not a contract. Nothing here is legally binding. Either side can walk away whenever.
- We reserve the right to deactivate any code that gets shared inappropriately (deal sites, posted to strangers, etc.). We'll DM you first if there's a problem.
- If you're under 18, you're welcome to be an ambassador, but please make sure your parent or guardian is okay with you posting about Good Kicks. We'll never ask you to sign anything. Commission payments to ambassadors under 18 will be sent to a parent's PayPal/Venmo account when applicable — we'll work that out with you when you hit the Repping tier.
