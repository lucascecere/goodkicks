# Townies Apparel Co. — Brand Guidelines

> Rooted in Massachusetts. Built for every town.
> Townies celebrates the places that shape us. Local pride. Timeless style.

**Brand:** Townies Apparel Co. · Massachusetts Towns · Est. 2024
**Category:** DTC local-pride apparel — starting with South Shore town hats.

---

## 1. Colors

| Token | Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `navy` | Navy | `#0D1B2A` | Primary text + UI, dark backgrounds |
| `forest` | Forest Green | `#2F4F3A` | Accents, CTAs, secondary fills |
| `cream` | Natural | `#F2EFE8` | Default page background |
| `stone` | Stone | `#8F918D` | Muted/secondary text, borders, dividers |
| `white` | White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed type, cards on dark, breathing room |

**Default pairing:** cream background, navy type, forest for action/accent, stone for muted detail. Navy and forest are roughly equal "dark anchors" — navy leads UI, forest leads brand accents.

---

## 2. Typography

| Role | Family | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Headlines | **College Block** (collegiate block) | Town names, big headlines, structural headers. The loud voice. |
| Secondary / Accent | **Script** (connected script) | Brand-level moments only — hero signature, footer, hang tags, "Townies" wordmark flourish. |
| Body | **Inter** | All running text, UI, captions, buttons. |

**Rule of thumb:** no more than two of these in one composition. Block + Inter for product pages; Script + Inter for brand/editorial moments. Don't mix block and script in the same headline.

---

## 3. Logo system & usage hierarchy

Variations in the kit: primary lockup (arched **TOWNIES** + MA shape + "APPAREL CO." banner), secondary script lockup, plus cream-bg, navy-bg, and green-bg versions.

**The single most important rule — town-as-hero:**
- On product cards and product pages, the **TOWN NAME is the hero** (display/block, large). The Townies wordmark is the **small trusted label** (like a woven tag), not the headline.
- This is how the brand stays distinctive instead of reading like a generic Massachusetts-pride brand. Townies is the label you trust; the town is what the customer is buying.

**When to use which:**
- **Script wordmark** → brand-level moments: header signature, footer, hang tags, packaging, "about" hero.
- **Block / primary lockup** → structural and merch contexts; reserve the collegiate *arch* (it's the most-copied format in this category — don't put it on everything).
- **MA state shape** → a *small* unifying accent and merch detail. Do **not** make the MA shape the hero of product UI; if every product leads with the state shape, towns stop feeling distinct.

**Backgrounds:** cream version is default; navy and green versions for dark/colored sections and merch.

- **MA monogram** — block "MA" lettering for compact spots.

---

## 5. Patterns & textures

Three brand patterns in the kit: a topographic contour-line pattern, a scattered MA-shapes pattern, and a navy speckle texture. Use **sparingly** — section backgrounds, packaging, hang-tag backs — at low opacity so they never fight the foreground. Never behind body text.

---

## 6. Brand voice

Rooted, proud, and a little self-aware — local pride without being corny. Short, declarative, confident. Talks about *places* and *people*, not "premium product." In on the joke of "townie" (affectionate, chip-on-shoulder), never sneering.

**Values:** Local Pride · Community · Quality · Authenticity · Tradition

---

## 7. Taglines

- Rep Your Town.
- From Massachusetts, For Massachusetts.
- Small Towns. Strong Roots.
- Your Town. Your Style.

Use as hero lines, section headers, merch copy. Keep them short and let them stand alone.

---

## 8. Photography direction

Heritage / "well-worn" / editorial — real people in real South Shore places (harbor, beach, rink, Main Street, a porch), warm natural light. Lifestyle over white-background catalog shots, especially on the homepage and editorial sections. Until a real shoot exists, components should degrade to styled placeholders (brand-color blocks or product-on-cream) so the build never depends on photography you don't have yet.

---

## 9. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Let the town be the loudest element on product UI.
- Keep layouts editorial and spacious (cream space is a feature).
- Use forest green for primary actions; navy for type.
- Reserve script for brand moments; reserve the collegiate arch for merch/structure.

**Don't**
- Lead every product with the MA state shape.
- Stack the arched collegiate lockup on every surface.
- Use more than two type families in one composition.
- Heavily tint or filter photography — keep it natural.
